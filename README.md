# 人當有孝順父母之實（DRS 431236542） / Ren dang you xiaoshun fumu zhi shi: Transcription Dataset

《人當有孝順父母之實》是 1847 年廈門刊行的華文基督教單張，上方總題「耶穌教之道」。文章從古代祀典談起，對照父母生前的奉養與死後供奉木主，批評以祭祀儀節代替孝行。篇末勸讀者把父母生平善狀詳記於族譜，供後代追念與效法。這份材料可用來研究晚清廈門傳教出版如何引用本地倫理與家族文書，重新解釋孝道和祖先祭祀。作者與出版者未詳。

本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 431236542 為底本，共取 1 幅固定影像，其中 1 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，30 項比對中保留 17 項未解。人工全文二校待完成。

Ren dang you xiaoshun fumu zhi shi is a Chinese Christian broadside printed in Xiamen in 1847, with the overall heading Yesujiao zhi dao. Beginning with ancient sacrificial rites, it contrasts caring for living parents with offerings to ancestral tablets after their deaths. It argues that rites cannot substitute for filial conduct and ends by proposing that families record their parents’ good deeds in genealogies for descendants to remember and emulate. The broadside documents how missionary print in late Qing Xiamen drew on filial ethics and family records to discuss ancestral rites. Its author and publisher are unidentified.

This dataset uses 1 fixed image from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 431236542. The main text is transcribed from 1 page. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription retains 17 unresolved comparison items. Full human collation is outstanding.

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 30 項比對、17 項未解及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。

底本為單張一幅。公開轉錄收錄上方總題、框內篇題、全篇 31 欄正文與左下刊記。折痕和局部疑字留定位。

The single-sheet transcription includes the overall heading above the frame, the article title, all 31 body columns, and the imprint at the lower left.

Claude 分別依固定影像完成初錄與獨立第二讀，再依原圖覆核程式列出的 30 項比對。採入 1 項有影像依據的更正，保留未解位置與候選。另有 2 項異讀保留原讀及候選。程式驗收確認公開 JSON、TXT 和 TEI 的逐欄內容一致。TEI 通過 P5 4.12.0 Relax NG。人工全文二校、專家審定和準確率量測待完成。

Claude produced separate readings from the fixed images and reviewed 30 comparison observations against the source images. The edition incorporates 1 correction supported by the images. The initial readings and alternatives remain recorded for 2 further proposed changes. Automated validation confirmed that the transcription content agrees column by column across JSON, TXT and TEI. The TEI passed P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation. Full human collation, expert assessment and accuracy measurement are outstanding.
